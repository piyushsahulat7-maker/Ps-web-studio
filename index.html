Real-time glass, in five terms.

Iridescence — A colour shift caused by thin-film interference, changing with angle and thickness.

Index of refraction — A measure of how much light changes direction as it enters a material.

Dispersion — The separation of light into colours because each wavelength bends differently.

Transmission — The amount of light that passes through a surface rather than being reflected or absorbed.

Roughness — The microscopic texture that spreads reflections from sharp highlights into soft light.

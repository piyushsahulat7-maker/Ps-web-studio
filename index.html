Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Fill out the Build Your Own Website form, review the generated summary, copy it if needed, open Instagram and send the order details to @piyush_web_studio_2026. Your order is received only after you send the DM.

Are the package photos real?

The ₹300 and ₹600 packages use AI-style/illustrative visuals by default. The ₹1,000 custom option can include real shop or product photos supplied by the customer. Photo editing or special image generation may change the quote.

Can I ask for a different design?

Yes. Use the custom form and describe your preferred colors, layout, pages and features. We will confirm whether it fits the selected package or needs a custom quote.

Can I ask for changes after the website is made?

Yes. Send the live website link and a clear list of changes through Instagram DM. The number of included revision rounds and any extra charges should be agreed before work begins.

Is a domain name included?

A free hosting link may be possible, depending on the hosting setup. A custom domain (like yourbusiness.com), paid hosting or paid third-party services are not automatically included and may cost extra.

Do you accept payments on this website?

This site currently collects requirements only; it does not take payments or automatically store orders. Payment method and any advance payment should be agreed in Instagram chat before work starts.

PS WEB STUDIO / MATERIAL NOTES

Five ideas behind the surface.

Iridescence

A shifting play of colour that changes with viewing angle and the way light meets a surface.

Index of refraction

A measure of how light changes direction as it passes from one material into another.

Dispersion

The separation of light into colours because different wavelengths bend by different amounts.

Transmission

The portion of light that travels through a material instead of reflecting from or being absorbed by it.

Roughness

The microscopic texture that softens reflections and turns sharp highlights into a wider glow.
